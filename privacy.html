Tome privacy policy

Last updated November 15, 2026.

Tome is a browser extension that replaces the new tab page. This policy explains what happens to your information.

What Tome keeps

Your board, notes, links, reminders, settings and other content are stored in your own browser on your own computer. If you choose a save or backup folder, copies are written to that folder. Tome has no servers and its developer never receives your content.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you connect Google Drive in Settings, Sync, Tome asks Google for permission to create and use its own files in your Drive (the "drive.file" permission). Tome cannot see any other file in your Drive. It writes the items you choose to sync, a list of your connected computers by the names you gave them, and daily copies of your Tome data, all in a folder named Tome in your Drive. These files go only between your browser and Google; the developer has no access to them.

The sign-in token Google gives Tome is stored in your browser. Disconnecting in Settings, Sync deletes it and tells Google to cancel it. You can also remove Tome's access at any time from your Google Account's security settings, and delete the Tome folder from your Drive.

The sign-in page at this site only passes Google's answer back to your browser. It stores nothing and sends nothing elsewhere.

Tome's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

For the weather, Tome sends your chosen location to public weather services to get the forecast. Site icons for your links are loaded by the browser. Shared add-ons and update notices are read from the project's public download page.

Sharing and selling

Tome does not sell, share or use your information for advertising, and it does not collect analytics.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Tome.
