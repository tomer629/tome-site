Tome

A new tab home base for Chrome and Edge.

Tome replaces the browser's new tab page with quick links, the weather, a task board, notes, a calendar with reminders and themes. Everything is kept in your own browser.

Tome can optionally sync chosen lists, links, reminders and contact names between your own computers through your own Google Drive. It only uses files it creates in a Tome folder in your Drive, and nothing goes to anyone else.
